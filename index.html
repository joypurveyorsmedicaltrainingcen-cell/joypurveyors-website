About Us

About Joy Purveyors

Joy Purveyors Medical Training Center serves Ndagani and the wider Chuka community with accessible outpatient and inpatient care, close to the main tarmac road towards Marine Park.

Joy Purveyors Medical Training Centre (JPMTC) is committed to training competent, ethical, and community-oriented health professionals. The Diploma in Community Health programme aims to produce graduates equipped with knowledge and skills to address emerging and existing public health challenges.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?

Not yet — insurance cover is coming soon. In the meantime, we accept cash and M-Pesa for all services.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept cash and M-Pesa (mobile money) for hospital services and school fees.

Is Joy Purveyors Medical Training Center accredited?

Yes. Our School of Health Sciences is fully accredited, and our programmes — including the Diploma in Community Health Nursing (DCHN), Health Services Support (HSS), and Perioperative Theatre Technology — meet the required national training standards.

What courses does the School of Health Sciences offer?

We currently offer a Diploma in Community Health Nursing (DCHN), Perioperative Theatre Technology and Health Services Support (HSS). Our September intake for nurses is ongoing now — call us to apply.

Do you offer both outpatient and inpatient care?

Yes. We offer general outpatient consultations as well as inpatient admission across our hospital beds, alongside dentistry, maternity, and laboratory/diagnostic services.

Where exactly are you located?

We're in Ndagani market, Chuka — about 150m from the tarmac road towards Marine Park.

Insurance cover: Not yet active — coming soon. Cash and M-Pesa are accepted for all services in the meantime.
